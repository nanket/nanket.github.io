Getting in

I forgot my pattern

On the unlock screen, tap Forgot Pattern? and fill in the 9 key words of your recovery story. You'll then set a new pattern.

Without the pattern or the story, nobody can open the vault, including us. There's no reset email because there's no account.

Where do I find my recovery story?

Open the Protect tab and tap Recovery Story. Kosha asks for your pattern first. Write the sentence down and keep it somewhere safe, away from your iPhone.

How do I turn on Face ID?

In Protect › Unlock, turn on Face ID. After your iPhone restarts, Kosha asks for your pattern once before Face ID works again.

Your vault

Is my vault backed up? What happens if I get a new iPhone?

Not yet. Your vault lives only on this iPhone, and it isn't included in iCloud or computer backups, so it doesn't move to a new iPhone and can't be restored if the phone is lost or erased.

Before you switch, reset or hand in your iPhone, open each item you want to keep and tap Export or Share. Don't delete the originals of anything you can't replace.

Can you recover my files?

No. Your files and keys never leave your iPhone, so we have nothing to recover from. That's what keeps them private.

Search or smart albums aren't finding things

Kosha reads new items in the background on your iPhone. Protect › Smart Search shows how far it has got. Search and smart albums work in English for now.

How do I delete everything?

Open Protect and tap Delete Everything. Every vault on the iPhone is erased and can't be opened again. Deleting the app does the same.

Kosha Pro

What does Pro add?

Kosha is free for up to 100 items. Pro removes the limit. If Pro ends, nothing is locked: every item stays viewable, exportable and deletable.

I bought Pro but it isn't showing

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account, then open Protect and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone, or manage them here. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
