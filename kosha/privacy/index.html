Kosha Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

Kosha is a private vault for your photos, videos, documents and notes. It has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. What you put in your vault stays encrypted on your iPhone, and we never see it.

Your vault

Photos, videos, documents, notes, album names and the search index are encrypted on your iPhone with keys that never leave it. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to open it.

Search and smart albums are worked out on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device Vision framework and an AI model that ships inside the app. Your photos are never uploaded to be analysed.

Permissions

  • Photos: to move the photos and videos you pick into the vault, to delete the originals when you ask, and to save an item back to Photos when you choose to.
  • Camera and microphone: for Kosha's private camera and document scanner. What you capture goes straight into the vault, never the camera roll.
  • Face ID: handled entirely by iOS. Kosha only learns whether the check passed; it never receives face data.

Purchases

Kosha Pro is sold through the App Store, so Apple processes your payment under Apple's privacy policy. We never see your payment details.

Kosha uses RevenueCat to check whether Pro is active. When the app starts, and when you buy or restore, RevenueCat receives a random ID created on your iPhone, your App Store purchase history for Kosha, and basic technical details such as the app and iOS versions, your App Store country and the network address of the request. This isn't linked to your name, email or Apple Account, and Kosha turns off RevenueCat's device-identifier collection and diagnostics.

Crash reports

Kosha has no crash reporting of its own. If you have chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us crash reports and app usage statistics under Apple's privacy policy. These never contain anything from your vault.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your vault exists only on your iPhone. It isn't included in iCloud or computer backups. Delete Everything in Kosha's Protect tab, or deleting the app, erases it for good.

To have the RevenueCat purchase record deleted, email us and we'll delete the records we can match. Apple keeps its own purchase records under its own policy.

Children

Kosha isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't collect personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions: nanket.dev@gmail.com.
