Kosha Privacy Policy for Android

Effective 29 October 2026

Kosha is a private vault for your photos, videos, documents and notes. It has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. What you put in your vault stays encrypted on your phone, and we never see it.

Your vault

Photos, videos, documents, notes, album names and the search index are encrypted on your phone with keys that never leave it and are tied to its secure hardware (Android Keystore). None of it is sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to open it.

Search and smart albums are worked out on your phone, using an AI model that ships inside the app. Your photos are never uploaded to be analysed.

When you type in Kosha, only the keyboard that came with your phone can type, and Kosha asks it to use incognito mode so it doesn't learn what you write.

Permissions

  • Camera and microphone: for Kosha's private camera. The microphone is asked for only when you switch to video. What you capture goes straight into the vault, never your gallery. Kosha has no location permission, so nothing it captures is tagged with where you were.
  • Fingerprint, face or screen lock: handled entirely by Android. Kosha only learns whether the check passed; it never receives fingerprint or face data or your screen-lock PIN.
  • No access to your photos or files: you add items with Android's photo picker or file picker, which hand Kosha only what you choose. Kosha can't delete the originals, so remove them in your gallery app if you want to.
  • Internet: used only to check and process Kosha Pro purchases, as described below.

Purchases

Kosha Pro is sold through Google Play, so Google processes your payment under Google's privacy policy. We never see your payment details.

Kosha uses RevenueCat to check whether Pro is active. When the app starts, and when you buy or restore, RevenueCat receives a random ID created on your phone, your Google Play purchase history for Kosha, and basic technical details such as the app and Android versions, your Google Play country and the network address of the request. This isn't linked to your name, email or Google Account. Kosha turns off RevenueCat's device-identifier collection and diagnostics, and doesn't use your phone's advertising ID.

Google Play's billing library, which Kosha needs to sell Pro, also sends Google technical data about the purchase flow. Kosha can't turn this off; Google handles it under its own policy.

Crash reports

Kosha has no crash reporting of its own. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google Play may send us crash reports and app performance statistics under Google's privacy policy. These never contain anything from your vault.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your vault exists only on your phone. It isn't included in Google backups or copied when you move to a new phone. Delete everything in Kosha's Protect tab, uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings erases it for good.

To have the RevenueCat purchase record deleted, email us and we'll delete the records we can match. Google keeps its own purchase records under its own policy.

Children

Kosha isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't collect personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions: nanket.dev@gmail.com.
