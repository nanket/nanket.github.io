Breeze Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

Breeze is a Mac utility that runs entirely on your computer. We built it so that your files never leave your Mac.

What Breeze does not collect

  • No account, no sign-up.
  • No analytics, telemetry, tracking or advertising identifiers.
  • No file names, file contents, app lists, browsing data or scan results are ever sent anywhere.

The only network requests Breeze makes

  1. License checks. When you activate Breeze Pro, and about once every two weeks after that, Breeze sends your license key and our product ID to Gumroad (api.gumroad.com) to confirm the purchase is valid. Gumroad's privacy policy applies to that request.
  2. Update checks. About once a day (you can turn this off in Settings), Breeze downloads a small file describing the latest version from our website. No personal information is included.
  3. App update checks (only when you open the Updater). Breeze asks each app's own update server whether a newer version exists, exactly as that app would itself.

Data stored on your Mac

  • Your license key, in your login Keychain.
  • Settings, in Breeze's preferences.
  • A history of items Breeze removed, in ~/Library/Application Support/Breeze/history.jsonl and ~/Library/Logs/Breeze/breeze.log. You can delete these at any time.

Crash reports

If Breeze crashes, it offers to open an email to us with the crash report attached. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email.

Payments

Purchases are handled by Gumroad, who acts as the merchant of record. We receive your email address and order details from Gumroad so we can provide support.
